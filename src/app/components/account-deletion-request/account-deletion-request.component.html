What happens when you submit a request

  • Submitting a request does not immediately delete your account. Edunexify reviews it, with your school where needed, and confirms that the request is yours.
  • If the request is approved, your sign-in is deactivated. We then assess, with your school, which of your personal data can be deleted or anonymised.
  • Some school records may need to be kept for legitimate educational, financial or legal reasons — for example fee and payment records, marks and report cards, and attendance history.
  • You can follow the status of your request on this page until your sign-in is deactivated. After that, we tell you the outcome by email.
