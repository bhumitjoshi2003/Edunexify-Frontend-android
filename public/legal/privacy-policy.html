Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

Edunexify (“Edunexify”, “we”, “us”) is a school management platform used by schools and by their administrators, teachers, students and parents, through the Edunexify website and the Edunexify Android app. This policy explains what personal data Edunexify processes, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can ask for your account to be deleted.

Your school decides how it uses Edunexify and is responsible for the school records it keeps in it, such as attendance, marks, fees and payments. Edunexify operates the platform and processes this data to provide the service to your school. For questions about your school's records, you can also contact your school directly.

Who this policy covers

  • School administrators and staff who manage the school in Edunexify.
  • Teachers, including teachers who check in for attendance from the app or website.
  • Students and parents whose accounts and records are created by their school.
  • Visitors to our website who ask us for a demo.

Accounts

Edunexify accounts are created by a school's administrators. You cannot register yourself in the app or on the website. Signing in uses secure, HTTP-only cookies. We keep a record of each signed-in session, including its IP address and device or browser type, so that you can review your active sessions and sign out of them. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash.

Information we process

  • Profile information entered by your school: name, user ID, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender and profile photo. For students, this can also include class and section, parents' names, school bus use and distance, joining and leaving dates, and leaving details recorded by the school. For teachers, it can include joining and leaving dates and exit details recorded by the school. For parents, it includes the link to their children.
  • School records: student and teacher attendance, leave applications and decisions, timetables, homework and classwork (including attached files), marks, results and report cards.
  • Teacher check-in location: see Location below.
  • Fees and payments: fee amounts, payment and refund records, and payment and order references from our payment provider. We do not receive or store full card details.
  • Messages and notifications: notices, in-app notifications, and the emails and push notifications we send for your school.
  • Support requests you send us from the app or website, including any screenshot you attach.
  • AI assistant conversations, where your school has enabled the Edunexify AI assistant: the messages you type and the assistant's replies.
  • Device and technical information: a push-notification token for your device, the app platform and version, IP addresses and device or browser type, and security records of important actions (for example sign-ins, changes to records and check-ins).
  • Demo requests from our website: your name, school name, email, phone number, city, approximate number of students and your message.

Location

Edunexify uses precise location only for teacher attendance, and only while the app or website is open: when a teacher taps Check in or Check out. The Android app may ask for location permission in advance when a teacher sets up attendance. Edunexify does not access location in the background.

  • Your position is compared with your school's configured location to confirm you are within the school's attendance area.
  • Check-in: the coordinates and the calculated distance from the school are stored with that day's attendance record. Your school's administrators can access them.
  • Check-out: the coordinates are used only for the check and are not stored.
  • The security record of each check-in includes the distance from the school, but not the coordinates.
  • If you do not allow location access, you cannot check in or out from the app or website; your school's administrator can record your attendance instead.

We have built an automatic process that clears stored check-in coordinates and distances 90 days after the check-in while keeping the attendance record itself. It is not switched on yet: it will be enabled once schools have approved it, and we will update this policy when it is. Until then, check-in coordinates are kept with the attendance record.

How we use information

  • To provide Edunexify to your school: accounts, attendance, leave, timetables, homework, marks and report cards, fees and payments, notices and the other features your school uses.
  • To confirm teacher attendance at the school's location.
  • To send emails and push notifications that you or your school need, such as account set-up, password resets, notices, attendance and fee reminders.
  • To process payments and refunds.
  • To answer support requests and demo requests.
  • To keep Edunexify secure: protecting accounts, keeping each school's data separate, recording important actions, and finding and fixing errors.
  • Where your school has enabled it, to provide the AI assistant.

We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Who we share information with

Your information is available to authorised people at your school according to their role, for example administrators and teachers. Parents can see the information about their own children that the school allows. We also use the following service providers to run Edunexify, and they receive only what they need for their service:

  • Database and file storage hosting: all Edunexify data, and files such as photos, report cards, homework attachments and support screenshots.
  • Brevo: sending emails (recipient address, name and the email content).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): delivering push notifications (device token and notification content).
  • Razorpay: processing fee payments and refunds. Payments are made to your school's Razorpay account where your school has set one up. Razorpay handles your card, UPI or bank details under its own privacy policy.
  • Sentry: error reports that help us find and fix faults. Our Sentry set-up is configured not to send default personal information and to remove request details, user details, error messages and other event context before a report is sent, keeping mainly the type of error, where in the code it occurred and a request reference. These safeguards reduce the personal information in error reports but cannot guarantee that none is ever included.
  • Better Stack: service health checks, which contain no personal data.
  • DeepSeek: generating AI assistant replies, where your school has enabled the assistant. Your messages and the school information needed to answer them are sent to DeepSeek.
  • OpenAI: searching documents that a school uploads to the AI assistant's knowledge base. The document text is sent to OpenAI.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect the safety of users or the security of the service.

Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies that are needed to keep you signed in. The website and app also store a few settings in your browser or device, such as your last selected class, timetable view or child, and whether you have seen product updates. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers.

How long we keep information

  • Security records are archived after 6 months and deleted after 2 years.
  • In-app notifications are deleted after 60 days.
  • AI assistant diagnostic records (conversations, tool use and errors) are deleted after 30 days. The assistant's short-term conversation memory expires after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • Teacher check-in coordinates are currently kept with the attendance record; see Location for the planned 90-day clearing.
  • School records, including attendance history, marks, report cards, fee and payment records, remain available across academic sessions, because schools need them after a student or teacher has left. Profile records and photos of students and teachers who have left are kept for the same reason. These are kept subject to the school's needs and any legal obligations that apply.
  • Other information, including session records, support requests and demo requests, does not yet have a fixed retention period. We will update this policy when one is set.
  • Backups may contain copies of information until those backups are deleted.

Deleting your account

You can ask for your Edunexify account to be deleted:

  • in the app or on the website: open the profile menu and choose Request account deletion; or
  • by email to support@edunexify.co.in, especially if you can no longer sign in. See Account deletion for details.

Each request is reviewed manually by our team. Nothing is deleted automatically, and submitting a request does not delete your account. We first confirm that the request comes from the account holder and, because your account belongs to your school, we consult your school. If the request is approved, your sign-in is deactivated, your active sessions are ended and your devices are removed from push notifications.

We then assess, with your school, which of your personal data can be deleted or anonymised and which must be kept. Information that forms part of the school's records may be retained for legitimate educational, financial or legal reasons, for example attendance history, marks and report cards, fee and payment records, and the student and teacher profiles and photos linked to those records, as may the security records described above. We therefore cannot promise that all of your personal data will be deleted. When we close your request, we record what was done and what was kept and why, and we tell you the outcome, or why the request could not be fulfilled. Copies in backups remain until those backups are deleted.

Security

Edunexify is served over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, sessions use secure HTTP-only cookies and can be ended at any time, each school's data is kept separate from other schools', and schools' payment gateway secrets are stored encrypted. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and limit access to it.

Children

Student accounts, including accounts for children, are created by schools for school purposes. Children cannot register themselves, and we use their information only to provide Edunexify to their school. Parents can contact the school or us about their child's information.

Your choices

  • Many profile details are managed by your school; ask your school to correct them.
  • You can review and end your active sessions, and change your password, in your account settings.
  • You can turn off location or notification permission in your device settings. Without location, you cannot check in or out from the app or website.
  • You can ask us about the personal data we hold about you, or ask for your account to be deleted, as described above.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the way we handle personal data changes, and change the date at the top.
